Reaction TestMe Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Joshua James Griggs-Humphries ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use Reaction TestMe (the "App"), a mobile application available on iOS and Android devices. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Who We Are

Reaction TestMe is developed and operated by Joshua James Griggs-Humphries, an independent developer based in New Zealand. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at privacy@josho.nz.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Reaction TestMe does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use the App.

Gameplay Data

The App collects and stores the following gameplay information to operate core features:

  • Reaction time scores and test results
  • In-app performance statistics
  • Game session data (e.g., number of attempts, accuracy metrics)
This data is used solely to display your progress and results within the App. It is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Advertising Data

The App displays advertisements served by third-party advertising partners. These partners may automatically collect certain technical and device information when ads are displayed, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address (may be used to infer approximate location)
  • Device type, OS version, and language settings
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., ad views, clicks)
  • App usage and performance data
This data is collected by our advertising partners directly and governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control how third-party advertisers collect or use this information.

Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising networks to display ads within the App. These providers may use cookies, SDKs, and similar tracking technologies to deliver personalised or interest-based advertising. Common advertising partners may include Google AdMob and similar networks. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies. You may opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking — disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android Settings › Google › Ads — enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"
    • Opting out of personalised ads does not stop all advertisements - you will still see non-personalised ads.

How We Use Information

We use the information described in this Privacy Policy for the following purposes:

  • To operate and improve the functionality of the App
  • To display your gameplay statistics and scores
  • To serve advertisements within the App
  • To analyse app performance and fix bugs or errors
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

  • With advertising partners to display ads within the App
  • With service providers who assist in operating the App, under confidentiality obligations
  • If required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public

Data Retention

Gameplay statistics and scores are stored locally on your device. You may delete this data at any time by uninstalling the App. We do not retain personal data on our own servers beyond what is necessary for operational or legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Reaction TestMe is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information. If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 16 years of age without appropriate parental consent.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Gameplay data is stored locally on your device, and the security of that data is also dependent on your device's own security settings.

Your Rights

As a New Zealand-based developer, we comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Under this Act, you may have the right to:

  • Access information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information where applicable
If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may also have rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@josho.nz.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Privacy Policy Complaints and Enquiries

If you have any queries or complaints about our Privacy Policy please contact us at privacy@josho.nz.
